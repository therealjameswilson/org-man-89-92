# Department of State Dispatch Sweep: 1991-1992 Leads

Checked: 2026-05-23

Scope: public Department of State *Dispatch* issues recovered through the
Internet Archive/Wayback Machine after the old DOSFAN host failed to resolve.

## Why This Matters

The original Current Policy sweep was concentrated in 1989-1990. The Dispatch
material helps fill the public-record side of the 1991-1992 gap. These are not
substitutes for internal State, NSC, OMB, or White House files, but they are
useful locators and scaffolding for mature-administration themes.

## Compiler Assessment

Use Dispatch as a locator set, not the evidentiary core. The best use is to:

1. Fill the 1991-1992 chronology.
2. Identify offices and programs to target in internal files.
3. Provide fallback/public text when archival originals are unavailable.

The strongest public additions to the existing Current Policy queue are the FY
1992/FY 1993 budget items, the 1991 International Cooperation Act/foreign
assistance law reform items, the 1992 NIS assistance-coordination items, and the
late-1992 Department reorganization item.
