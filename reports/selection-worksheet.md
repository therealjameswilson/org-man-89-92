# Selection Worksheet

Checked: 2026-06-01

Purpose: turn the project notes into document-slot decisions. This is the
compiler-facing triage board: what to promote, what to hold pending archival
review, what should serve only as source-note support, and what should not
consume a document slot unless the final architecture changes.

After sending request batches, track responses in the
[request tracking ledger](request-tracking-ledger.html).

Spreadsheet export: [selection worksheet CSV](selection-worksheet.csv).

## Status Key

- Promote: strong enough to review as a selected document now, subject to
  source-note verification.
- Hold: important lane, but the current evidence is a locator or public
  fallback; do not select until the archival/control record is reviewed.
- Support: use in source notes, chronology, editorial notes, or context rather
  than as a selected document.
- Selective: include only if the final chapter architecture needs that lane.
- Drop for now: do not spend compiler time unless a new source changes the
  evidentiary value.

## Immediate Decisions To Log

| Decision | Why it matters | Recommended default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can January 19, 1993 NSD 79 be selected in a 1989-1992 volume? | It is the best covert-action procedure anchor but falls after December 31, 1992. | Treat as Bush administration capstone unless the compiler enforces a hard terminal date. |
| Does the intelligence chapter get three document slots? | PDB process, covert action/congressional notification, and intelligence budget are distinct management questions. | Yes, if primary records are released enough to support selection. |
| Is NSC procedure an opening frame or a chapter? | Too many procedural documents can crowd out management decisions. | Use as opening frame unless a document shows a concrete management decision. |
| Are public budget speeches selectable? | They are easy to cite but weak as final FRUS organization/management evidence. | Use as fallback or editorial chronology; prefer internal OMB/State/White House records. |
| How many case studies belong? | Case studies can pull the volume away from institutional management. | One or two maximum, only where machinery is visible. |

## Evidence Standard Before Promotion

Do not mark a candidate as selected until these fields are known or explicitly
not available:

| Field | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Control copy | Repository, series, folder/file title, OA/ID or NAID, and whether the file is the signed/circulated/final copy. |
| Document identity | Date, title, document type, originator/drafter, recipient, meeting time/place if relevant, and attachments. |
| Classification | Classification and handling markings as printed on the document; do not infer from a public reprint. |
| Release status | Fully open, partially open, withheld, referred, public fallback, or not located. |
| Selection value | The document must show organization, management, coordination, budget, staffing, authority, oversight, or implementation machinery. |
| Source-note readiness | Draft source note follows the [source-note audit](source-note-style-audit.html) and any bracketed facts are resolved or explained. |
