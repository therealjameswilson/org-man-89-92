# NSC Organization and Administrative Source Review

Checked: 2026-05-23

Sources sampled:

- `Reagan-Bush Transition Briefing Book: Background on NSC Organization and
  Administration`, NAID 470760855.
- `National Security Council Administrative Manual`, NAID 470760856.

## Finding

These files are important source anchors, but most of their value is contextual.
They should support the volume's source notes and institutional framing. Only
specific pages or directives should be selected as documents if the volume
includes an explicit NSC process chapter.

## Transition Briefing Book: NAID 470760855

The OCR sample identifies the briefing book as the first NSC transition volume.
It documents:

- Current NSC staff organization.
- Division of responsibilities among the National Security Advisor's office and
  directorates.
- Existing interagency process.
- NSC budget, staffing, and administration.

The table of contents includes:

- NSC fact sheet.
- Organizational diagrams.
- NSDD 276, `National Security Council Interagency Process`.
- NSC interagency groups.
- NSC organization.
- Secretariat.
- White House Situation Support Staff.
- Administration.
- NSC budget/personnel information.

Compiler use: cite for inherited Reagan-to-Bush NSC machinery. Consider
selecting NSDD 276 or a transition cover memo only if the volume needs a formal
opening document on inherited NSC process.

## Administrative Manual: NAID 470760856

The administrative manual is an internal procedures file. The sampled table of
contents and tabs cover:

- Conference rooms.
- NSC directory and organization.
- Disposition of chronological files.
- Standards of conduct and Hatch Act.
- Consultant procedures.
- Inprocessing and outprocessing.
- Whereabouts listings.
- Travel, passport, visa, and administrative forms.
- Records management and staff chron/convenience files.
- White House Situation Support Staff and Secretariat practices.

Compiler use: source-note context, not likely a selected document. Its strongest
value is explaining how NSC staff records, chron files, Secretariat routing, and
administrative controls worked.

## Selection Decision

For now:

- Keep NAID 470760855 in the high-priority review queue.
- Keep NAID 470760856 as a source anchor.
- Do not spend selection slots on routine administrative manual pages unless a
  chapter specifically discusses NSC records, paper flow, or staff controls.
- Pull the NSC Secretariat Procedure Manual and NSC Procedural Manual binders if
  the outline includes a process chapter.
