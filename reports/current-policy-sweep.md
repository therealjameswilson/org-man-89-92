# Current Policy Sweep: FRUS 1989-1992, Volume II

Checked: 2026-05-26

Official volume: *Foreign Relations of the United States, 1989-1992, Volume II,
Organization and Management of Foreign Policy*

Official page: [FRUS 1989-1992, Volume II](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1989-92v02)

Official status: Being Researched

Source folder: [Google Drive Current Policy folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1EzQwduz85YtC1mucT0IrYZRD1LRuJvEy)

## Scope

The folder contains six Department of State *Current Policy* PDF compilations,
not a broad set of declassified files. The useful material is concentrated in
1989-1990 budget, administration, refugee, international organization, and
coordination statements. The folder does not appear to include 1991-1992
*Current Policy* material, so it cannot by itself cover the full 1989-1992
period. The assessment below is scoped to the official Volume II subject,
Organization and Management of Foreign Policy.

## Likely Exclude

Most other pamphlets in the folder are topical foreign policy speeches rather
than organization and management records. They may be useful in regional FRUS
volumes, but they do not appear to add much to a volume centered on Department
organization, budget, personnel, foreign assistance management, refugee
processing, international organization arrears, or interagency/program
coordination.

Examples to deprioritize here include regional assistance statements, fisheries,
narcotics policy speeches unless tied to UN coordination, business/economic
diplomacy speeches, Gulf crisis statements, and general Europe/Latin America
policy addresses.

## Bottom Line

The must-review inclusions from this folder are CP 1147, CP 1245, CP 1256,
CP 1163, CP 1146, and CP 1277. The most defensible selective additions are
CP 1171, CP 1219, CP 1289, CP 1290, CP 1307, and CP 1309, depending on how
broadly the volume treats international organizations, assistance coordination,
refugees, export controls, and policy-process mechanisms.
