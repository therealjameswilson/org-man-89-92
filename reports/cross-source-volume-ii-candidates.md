# Cross-Source Sweep: FRUS 1989-1992, Volume II

Checked: 2026-05-22

Official volume: *Foreign Relations of the United States, 1989-1992, Volume II,
Organization and Management of Foreign Policy*

Official page: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1989-92v02

## Method

This pass checked source anchors and harvested records cited in the other local
FRUS companion repositories, plus the published FRUS 1989-1992, Volume XXXI
sources page. The goal was to find documents and source pools that should be
reviewed for Volume II, not to re-score every regional policy document.

The strongest matches came from:

- National Security Council Institutional Files, Transition Files, NAID 348937136.
- H-Files National Security Review and National Security Directive files.
- H-Files NSC and NSC/Deputies Committee meeting files.
- White House Chief of Staff, Cabinet Affairs, and Policy Development file-unit
  leads surfaced by the broader Bush textual collections sweep.
- State Department lot files cited in the published Volume XXXI sources page,
  especially Baker, Eagleburger, Kimmitt, Ross, and Zoellick files.

## President Bush Chief-of-Mission Letter

The strongest single field-management candidate surfaced in this pass is
President Bush's July 12, 1990 letter of instruction to all U.S. Chiefs of
Mission. It should be checked for inclusion as a final-version document.

Source trail:

- *State*, October 1990, pp. 2-3, says the White House letter had gone to all
  Chiefs of Mission and prints the President's text.
- The same article reports that the letter was cleared by State, Agriculture,
  Commerce, Defense, Justice, Transportation, Treasury, AID, CIA, JCS, OMB, and
  USIA before distribution.
- The State Department's *Digest of United States Practice in International Law
  1989-1990* is cataloged publicly by data.gov. Search-index snippets identify
  an "Ambassadorial Functions: Authority" entry for this letter. The data.gov
  record points to `http://www.state.gov/documents/organization/139393.pdf`; as
  of this check, that State PDF URL returns an HTML error page, not a usable
  PDF.
- NARA NAID 470760858 contains a September 14, 1989 Bush memorandum and a
  country-specific letter to Ambassador Michael Armacost in Tokyo. That version
  is useful for drafting history and source context, but it is not the final
  all-chief-of-mission text printed in October 1990.

Editorial recommendation: treat the July 12, 1990 all-chief-of-mission letter as
the inclusion target. Use NAID 470760858 as a related source file and check the
Bush Library/White House records, S/S files, Central Foreign Policy File, and
Office of Chief of Mission Authority and Overseas Staffing files for the signed
or circulated final copy.

## Low-Yield Source Sets

The presidential memcon/telcon chronologies and regional directorate source
files cited in the other companion repos are mostly topical. They are still
valuable for regional volumes, but they did not surface many Volume II
organization/management candidates beyond the process-specific NSC/DC files
listed above.

For Volume II, use those records only when a document is about machinery:
budgeting, interagency review, public diplomacy coordination, refugee admissions
administration, Chief of Mission authority, or NSC-State process.
