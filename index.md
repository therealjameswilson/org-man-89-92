## Compiler Workflow

1. [Candidate control center](reports/compiler-control-center.html): selection
   posture, blocking evidence, and next actions.
2. [Research requests](reports/research-request-packet.html) and the
   [request ledger](reports/request-tracking-ledger.html): proposed pulls and
   recorded responses. "Not recorded" does not establish that a request was never sent.
3. [Response triage](reports/response-triage-sheet.html) and
   [source-note worklist](reports/source-note-worklist.html): document identity,
   copy status, markings, and missing provenance.
4. [Compiler decisions](reports/compiler-decision-log.html) and
   [promotion gate](reports/promotion-gate-checklist.html): resolve selection
   questions before treating a candidate as final.
